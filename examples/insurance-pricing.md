# Motor insurance pricing

Fictional annual premiums for an internal AgentSim demonstration. All amounts are in EUR. This document does not describe actual products or agreements with the named partners.

## Policy

Use the model's annual base premium for the demonstration's Comprehensive cover. Apply the selected partner's discount as a percentage of the base premium. Round the final premium to two decimal places. The discount amount is the difference between the base and final premium. Taxes and extra fees are outside this example.
