Fictional training policy: Third-party liability pays for eligible injury or property damage you cause to other people, not damage to your own car. Collision pays for eligible damage to your own car in an accident. Theft and fire pays for eligible loss caused by theft or fire. Roadside assistance offers help after a breakdown; it is not accident-repair cover. A deductible is the policyholder's contribution to an eligible claim: an eligible repair of EUR 1,200 with a EUR 200 deductible leaves EUR 1,000 for the insurer to pay. Eligibility, limits and exclusions depend on the policy terms. This is a made-up training example, not an actual insurance product or advice. Use only these supplied facts.
